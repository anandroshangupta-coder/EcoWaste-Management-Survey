What is e-waste

Electronics don't disappear — they accumulate.

E-waste is any discarded device with a plug, battery, or circuit board: phones, laptops, chargers, TVs, printers. When it's burned, buried, or dumped instead of processed properly, it leaches lead, mercury, and cadmium into soil and water — and the recoverable copper, gold, and rare-earth metals inside it are lost for good.

01 · Scale

It's the fastest-growing waste stream

Every upgrade cycle — a new phone, a replaced router — adds to a pile that most municipal systems still aren't built to handle.

02 · Risk

Informal disposal is toxic

Open burning to recover metal releases heavy metals and dioxins, harming the people doing the burning first.

03 · Opportunity

It's also a resource

A tonne of circuit boards holds more gold than a tonne of ore. Proper recycling recovers material, not just removes waste.

Disposal guidance

What to do — and what not to do

✕ Don't

  • 🔥 Burn electronics to recover metal — releases toxic fumes.
  • 🔋 Throw batteries into normal household garbage.
  • 🗑 Dump electronics in open areas, drains, or landfills.
  • 🔨 Manually break open devices for parts without protection.

✓ Do

  • 🛠 Repair devices before replacing them.
  • ♻ Reuse or repurpose working components.
  • 🎁 Donate devices that still function.
  • 🏷 Sell responsibly through verified refurbishers.
  • ✅ Hand e-waste to authorized recycling channels.
